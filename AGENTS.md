# Agents

The starting point for any coding agent working in this repository: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any other.

- **What comes next:** [docs/superpowers/roadmap.md](docs/superpowers/roadmap.md), in order, with the spec or plan each step starts from. Designs live in `docs/superpowers/specs/`, plans in `docs/superpowers/plans/`.
- **Crete levels:** the rules any new or changed level must keep (the difficulty gate, every piece having a purpose a player sees, a beast that is a real threat, the ramp, the gentler band for hidden walls, teaching first, new layouts, real quotes) are in [the Crete README's "Rules for new levels"](websites/www.cs/games/crete/README.md#rules-for-new-levels). In `websites/www.cs/games/crete/test/`, `difficulty.test.ts` enforces the gate, every piece having a purpose, the live beast, the ramp and the hidden-wall band; each `chapter-N.test.ts` its teaching levels and pages; `campaign.test.ts` the quotations; `fewest.test.ts` the fewest moves; `layouts.test.ts` the new layouts; `endless-bank-0.test.ts` to `-3` Endless's gate on every level of each difficulty's bank, a quarter each; `endless-gate.test.ts` the banks' cover of every tier, family and hidden walls and their climb; `rating.test.ts` the habit players, the difficulty score and the open-space and tool rules; `rating-agree.test.ts` that the editor's rating and Endless's gate agree; `engine-same-0.test.ts` to `-3` the solver's exact answers on every campaign and bank level (a new or rebuilt level is recorded with `tools/engine-same.ts record <shard>`).
- **Each site and game** has its own README: start from [README.md](README.md).
- **Branches:** the main checkout stays on `main`; work happens on a branch in its own git worktree, run with its own compose project (`COMPOSE="docker compose -p <name> -f compose.yaml" make up` from the site's folder) so it never takes another checkout's containers or ports.
- **Deploy:** merging to `main` deploys www.cs, and Crete's shared Endless bank to happystl.com, automatically (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, main's tip only). Never run `make deploy` by hand, and never retry or poll SSH or sftp to www.cs.siue.edu: repeated logins get the address banned. If a deploy fails, ask before trying again.
- **Words on screen:** buttons, menus, tabs and headings are in Title Case ("New Game"); sentences stay in sentence case. The website never mentions make, this repository or password managers.
- **Writing voice** (the owner's rules, 2026-11-14), for words a player or reader sees and for anything written in the owner's voice: running prose is whole, correctly punctuated sentences, with related ideas joined by a colon or a semicolon, never clipped into fragments for punch. Slightly informal is welcome and contractions are fine, except in white papers and technical papers, which take none. Keep sentences and paragraphs short. Buttons, menus, headings, labels and captions are not sentences and need not be. Titles of works are italic wherever the medium allows it (*The Island of Crete*, *Operation Tankity*, *The CIC*); buttons, menu entries and alt text stay plain.
- **Docs describe the current state**, not its history.
- **No secrets:** the repository is public.

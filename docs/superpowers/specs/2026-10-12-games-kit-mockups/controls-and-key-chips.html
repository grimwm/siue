The whole game, every button (v10: dark keycaps)

Key chips are one light keycap style on every button (gold, dark or red), centred with their label. Menu has its chip inline (A); deck buttons stack it under the word. Ammo lives in the status bar; the Weapon button shows damage and blast size. Phones have no key chips.

Every control shows an icon and a word, so nothing depends on hover tooltips (which phones never show). Aim, Drive and Fire are separate groups, so the two pairs of left/right arrows can't be confused. Radio, Next song and Help stay one tap away; less-used ones move into the Menu.

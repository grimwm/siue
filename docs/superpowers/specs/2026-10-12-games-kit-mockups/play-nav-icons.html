Play page top bar: icon buttons

Games, Install and Share get icons. On desktop each icon sits beside its word. On phones each becomes a 40 px square icon button, and its name is still read by screen readers and shown as a tooltip. Each option is drawn at 1280 px and at three Samsung widths: 360, 384 and 412 px (the S Ultras are 384–412). A red note marks a width where the bar would wrap.

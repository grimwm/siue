    How to play

    • Reach the exit 🚪 alive. The Minotaur 👹 moves twice per move you make.
    • Safe zones 🛡️ block the Minotaur. Catch your breath there — but lingering beside it still burns 2 HP a turn.
    • Barriers 🚧 block corridors. Grab a 🔌 fuse and walk into one to blow it open (+40). Nothing ever pauses.
    • Bash (Space) shoves the adjacent Minotaur backwards. It costs your turn and needs 3 turns to recharge.
    • Grab tools 🔮🥣📻 and script pages 📄. Three pages earn the True Ending.
    • Stuck? Hint (H) flashes one step. Answer path (V) reveals the whole route. Play-through (T) plays for you. M toggles sound.

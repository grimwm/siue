How to Play

  1. Flip Game On in the top bar.
  2. Move the mouse to dodge bolts and nukes — the eye and drones track your cursor.
  3. Click drones to KO them and score.
  4. Grenade (G): arm, then click where you want the blast.
  5. Raptor (R): orbital strike that clears the field and dazes the eye.
  6. Over time, expect occasional nukes and smaller missiles (each missile costs 2 HP).
  7. Cyan/pink missiles chase you; orange ones aim where you just were.
  8. Go 10 seconds without taking a hit to heal 1 HP.
  9. More drones appear as your KO count rises.
  10. You start with 30 HP — hit 0, or take a nuke, then enter 3 initials for the board.

# Credits

Every effect sprite sheet in `sprites/` is generated by `blender/render_fx.py`
from procedural Blender scenes (the sources are the `.blend` files beside it;
the shells atlas keeps one scene per shell in `blender/shells/`). The fire and
smoke (`blast`, `plume`, `dirt`, `flakburst`, `nuke`) are Cycles volumes shaped
by noise; the shells in flight are modelled from primitive meshes.
No external images, textures or models are used. The moon is a procedural
sphere (noise maria, Voronoi craters); no NASA or other lunar texture is
included.

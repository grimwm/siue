# Credits

Every effect sprite sheet in `sprites/` is generated by `blender/render_fx.py`
from procedural Blender scenes (the sources are the `.blend` files beside it;
the shells atlases keep one scene per shell in `blender/shells/`,
`blender/shells_blast/`, `blender/shells_beams/`, `blender/shells_earth/` and `blender/shells_fire/`). The fire and
smoke (`blast`, `plume`, `dirt`, `flakburst`, `nuke`, `flame`) are Cycles volumes shaped
by noise; the shells in flight are modelled from primitive meshes.
No external images, textures or models are used. The moon is a procedural
sphere (noise maria, Voronoi craters); no NASA or other lunar texture is
included. The parachute is procedural too (a striped half-sphere canopy and
cylinder lines). The `beam` (a stretched glowing core and halo with curve
filaments) and `plasma` (a noise-displaced torus, a thin leading ring and
curve filaments) sheets are emissive meshes rendered with EEVEE.

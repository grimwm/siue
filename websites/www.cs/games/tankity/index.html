    How to play

    • Scorched-earth artillery: you and the drone battery take turns lobbing shells across destructible hills. Last one rolling wins the round.
    • Hold ◀▶ for barrel angle, ▲▼ for power. A/D drives while you have fuel. Ctrl fires and ends your turn.
    • Wind pushes every shell, so check the meter before you fire. Q cycles Shell, Buckshot, Mortar, Rail, and the round-4 NUKE. Shells are free; the good stuff is bought with winnings, starting with a $600 stake you spend in the shop before round 1.
    • There is no final victory. Every round the battery rebuilds with thicker armor, steadier aim, and deeper magazines. How many hills can you hold?
    • F toggles fullscreen for the full hillside view.
    • E toggles sound effects, M toggles the music. Each has its own button in the menu (C opens it, ESC closes menus), so one can stay on while the other rests. New Game, seed, fullscreen, rooms (O), and random hills (T) live there too.
    • 1 through 4 load a favorite shell (hold Shift with a number to pin the loaded shell to that slot). In the shop, ▲/▼ move, ◀/▶ pick how many packs to buy (never more than the chest covers), B or Enter buys, number keys buy that row, and P or V previews it.
    • Explosions carve craters and damage by distance. Direct hits pay double. You have 3 lives; every 3000 points earns a 1-up (max 5).
    • The battery (Reaper, Wraith, Spotter) aims back, and gets steadier every round. Keep moving and mind the wind.
    • Rooms lets friends share a hillside: host a room, read out the code, and take turns with the battery filling empty seats. Initials only, no chat, and the server settles every shot so nobody can fudge a hit. The host can pick named hills, and a rematch replays them without ever revealing the seed.
    • Opening the page runs a demo battle. Press New Game (or N) to take the controls with a fanfare. Every round gets its own name card before the shop and the shooting.
    • The camera pulls back as the battle spreads so every tank stays in view. The railgun trades its old cross-map glare for a shorter, saner bolt.
    • L shows or hides the radio log, H this help, R the after-action report, and ESC closes any open menu. Overlays never pause the battle.

        Game rooms

        Host a room and read the 4-letter code to your friends, or join theirs. Everyone picks 3-letter initials (kept clean, lowercase is fine). There is no chat, only artillery. Empty seats go to the drone battery, and a quiet crew fires on its own after a while so no room ever stalls. The host may also pick named hills, like Canyon 3, so the crew can replay a favorite without ever seeing its seed.

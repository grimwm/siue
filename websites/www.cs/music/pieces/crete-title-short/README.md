# Crete: title theme (short)

The one-minute version of the Crete title theme: Ariadne's tune once on the
lyre, one organ swell, then the drone alone, returning to the start. It is the
same music as the title theme's draft 1. Source:
`music/compose/pieces/crete_title_short.py`.

Make the audio with `make music-render PIECE=crete-title-short VERSION=1`, then
publish it with `make music-publish PIECE=crete-title-short`.

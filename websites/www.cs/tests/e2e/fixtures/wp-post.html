A small game lands on the blog

Posted on November 4, 2026 by William

Welcome back to the blog. This week a little visitor from campus moved in: a scanner eye that watches the page, and a fleet of drones that would very much like to take it apart.

Press the button below to start it. The page is the battlefield: every word on it is fair game, and when the first nuke lands the letters scatter across the screen.

Why put a game on a blog?

Because it is fun, and because it shows what a few hundred lines of TypeScript can do to a page that was never designed for it. Nothing here is permanent: leave the game and the page comes back exactly as it was.

So say we all.
